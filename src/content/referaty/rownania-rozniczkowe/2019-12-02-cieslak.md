Referat pt.

One-dimensional fully parabolic quasilinear system of chemotaxis
with critical diffusion.

Streszczenie: In my talk I will speak about the system of two
one-dimensional parabolic
equations of Keller-Segel type with nonlinear diffusion of cells. I
will be mainly
focused on critical diffusion, criticality can be viewed via scaling
or (un)boundedness of a natural Liapunov functional related to the problem.
I will show that unlike in higher dimensions, in the case of critical
diffusion all solutions (independently on the size of initial mass) are
globally-in-time defined and they stay bounded. In higher dimensions one can
find such a value of mass m\_\* s.t. for initial mass of cells smaller than
m\_\* bounded solutions exist, while for initial mass of cells greater than m\_\*
solutions blowup in finite-time for sufficiently concentrated data.
The talk is based on two papers (T.Cieślak, K. Fujie, Proc. AMS 2018) and
(B. Bieganowski, T.Cieślak, K.Fujie, T.Senba, Math. Nachr. 2019).
