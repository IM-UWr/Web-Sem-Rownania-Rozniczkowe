The Kolmogorov theory contained in the 3 celebrated articles (1941) is in
some way the starting point for all turbulence models. However, the
predictions contained in this model and corrections to it have not been
proved to be true or false rigorously, the problem being very complex
(studying Navier-Stokes in 3D is one of the Clay Millenium problems).

Here we are concerned with he most well-known simplified model for the 3D
Navier-Stokes equation: the Burgers equation (in 1d, multi-d and with
fractional dissipation). For this model we get sharp estimates for the
Sobolev norms and for small-scale qualities (Fourier spectrum, increments)
which are relevant for the theory of turbulence.
All these results are optimal: the upper and lower estimates coincide up to
a multiplicative constant when the viscosity coefficient goes to 0.
