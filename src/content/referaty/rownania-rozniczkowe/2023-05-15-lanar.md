The parabolic-parabolic Keller-Segel system (with a parameter  
characterising the speed of propagation of the signal) plays a key  
role in the mathematical study of chemotaxis.  
We give conditions for well-posedness for this system and its toy  
models, as a function of this small parameter.  
Conversely, we obtain almost sharp solutions which explode for toy models.

This is a collaboration with P. Biler (UWr) and L. Brandolese (Lyon).
