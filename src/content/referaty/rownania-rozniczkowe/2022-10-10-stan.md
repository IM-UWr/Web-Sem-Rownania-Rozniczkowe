In this talk I will present a nonlocal version of the porous medium  
equation. I will explain the existence of a class of weak solutions  
and some properties of solutions like space-time decay, asymptotic  
behavior for large times, speed of propagation. This is a joint work  
with Juan Luis Vázquez and Félix del Teso (Universidad Autonoma de  
Madrid).
