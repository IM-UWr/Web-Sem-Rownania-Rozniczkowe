Critical points of variational functionals describe a wide
range of real
life phenomena. The typical approach to tackle their properties goes via
the limits of families of approximate problems. Such a procedure may
encounter difficulties that are impossible to by-pass within the
proposed model. This affects existence, regularity, problems with
adjusting numerical schemes etc. I will discuss approximation in
unconventional function spaces that is useful in the calculus of
variations, focusing on when and why it may fail.
This is based on a couple of projects with M. Borowski, P. Bousquet, F.
De Filippis, B. Lledos, B. Miasojedow published recently (JFA 2022,
Nonl. Anal. 2024, Calc. Var. PDEs 2024, arxiv:2410.14995).
