We study semilinear problems in general bounded open sets
for non-local operators with exterior and boundary conditions, where
the operators are more general than the fractional Laplacian. We also
give results in case of bounded $C^{1,1}$ open sets. The talk is based
on joint work with Ivan Biočić and Zoran Vondraček.
