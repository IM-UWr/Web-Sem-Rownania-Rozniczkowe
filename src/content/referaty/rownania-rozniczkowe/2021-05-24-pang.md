In this talk I shall discuss the well-posedness of a class of SDE  
with irregular random drift. Well-posedness of SDEs with random  
coefficients, though not as well-understood as SDEs with deterministic  
coefficients, have been explored in different cases. I shall give some  
background on the subject, and present new techniques leading to a new  
result, motivated by studies on a stochastic Hunter--Saxton equation.  
This is joint work with Helge Holden (NTNU) and Kenneth H. Karlsen  
(Oslo).
